I'm super happy with the project, and progress, my group has made too. Our project is genuinely super useful to me - I can see so much value in being able to predict my final grades accurately. I want to add a few more features though, especially one to change the weights.

One thing I've noticed is super valuable in the presentations I've watched is *confidence* in your expertise. Especially in Linus's presentation, having seen his actual talks, I know he's a fairly awkward speaker but in conversation he does great. Being able to adapt that mentality to any talk is valuable.
