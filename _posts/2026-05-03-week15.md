It's the last week of OSSD, and the last week of the semester. As such, it's also the last post I'll be making to this blog.

This post will be entirely my reflections on this course, especially on the final project. I think it was a strong way to close, and I'm really proud of what we did.

<!--more-->

Nanobot was an incredibly fun project to contribute to. My teammates - Leo, Alan, and Rohit - were incredibly helpful to work with, and I feel like I gained quite a bit from building alongside them. I remember when I struggled setting up my nanobot OLlama instance, Alan was able to help me fix it in minutes. Leo was invaluable in making the presentation come to life, and Rohit was consistently helpful whenever I needed a second pair of eyes. Our meetings were fruitful and efficient, and I'm honestly excited to see where my team goes in the future.

Another benefit of this project is that I'll actually be USING nanobot in the future. It's such a cool piece of software, and considering how cheap Deepseek-V4 is, I've been able to fully automate so many routine tasks (eg adding stuff to my calendar, applying to jobs) with simple LLM commands. Incredibly useful, and something I'm glad I have exposure to.

I do wish our presentation had gone better, though. We ran out of time and didn't get to fully discuss our highlights and favourite moments and frustrations - unfortunate, because I was really looking forward to complaining about the _one hour_ turnaround time for the last PR I made. Regardless, I feel like I delivered my part well, and I do always love presenting (and talking about stuff I made).

Overall, this has been an incredibly rewarding class, and I'm so glad I got to actually contribute to open-source. It feels like I've been a bit outside the open-source community for a bit, and it's nice getting back into it. 

Thanks for reading my blog this semester, and I'm so glad to have had this experience!
