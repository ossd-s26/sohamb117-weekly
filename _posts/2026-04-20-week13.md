We spend this week's class going over open source projects that are _not_ open source code. 

<!--more-->

My project of choice was OpenCola, an attempt to produce open-source Coca-Cola using relatively accessible resources. It was released in 2001 by author Cory Doctorow, and has been iterated on several times. It was then spun into a nonprofit that took the name OpenCola, that sold the cola to fund their own open-source projects and bring awareness to open-source projects at large. Because it was released under GNU GPL, some brands have started selling canned OpenCola for profit. OpenCola, the original nonprofit, shut down in 2003, and as such, the project has more or less stopped development. Other projects I found interesting were OpenStax, the open-source textbook publishing platform, and OpenSanctions, which I thought was super interesting. Nice to know I have no sanctions on me.

This week, I got my first PR merged into Nanobots! It's quite simple, and it just adds support for LM Studio models and also nullable API keys. It was really rearding to get that merged in, especially since I am using the feature right now.
